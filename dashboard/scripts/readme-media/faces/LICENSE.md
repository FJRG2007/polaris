# Fixture faces

The pictures in this folder are the profile photos and camera feeds of the
invented people in the README scenes, one pair per fixture account id
(`fixtures/people.ts`): `<id>.webp` is the square profile photo, and
`<id>.camera.webp` is the same photo framed as a webcam, which the call scenes
play as that person's camera. A person with no file here is drawn with their
initials, as an account with no photo is.

They are stock photos of models, not of anybody named in the fixtures. Each was
downloaded from its Unsplash page, cropped and resized to small WebP:
