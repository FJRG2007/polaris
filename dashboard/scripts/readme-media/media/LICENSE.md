# Fixture files

What the fixture messages send, and the pictures of the fixture spaces and
organization, named by the fixture id that carries them (`fixtures/chat.ts`,
`fixtures/people.ts`):

- `*.webp` are pictures of Polaris itself, scaled down from the README's own
  screenshots in `docs/assets/media`, standing in for the screens and the call
  recording the team shares.
- `*.svg` are the space's custom emoji, and the marks of the invented spaces and
  of the Northwind organization (served as their pictures), all drawn for this
  repository. None of them is, or imitates, a real brand.

All of them are part of this repository and under its license.
