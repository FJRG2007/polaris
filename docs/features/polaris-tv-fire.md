# Polaris TV on Fire TV

[Leer en español](polaris-tv-fire.es.md) - [Back to the README](../../README.md)

On Fire TV (Fire OS 6 or newer), Polaris TV is an app you open: Amazon keeps the Home button, and Fire OS
does not hand it to any other launcher. The 2015 Fire TV box on Fire OS 5 refuses the install altogether.

## Install

1. On the Fire TV, open Settings > My Fire TV > About and press OK on the device name seven times, until
   Developer options appears.
2. In Settings > My Fire TV > Developer options, turn on **ADB debugging**.
3. On a computer on the same network, open the Polaris desktop app, go to Downloads > Polaris TV > Install
   on a TV, leave "Make it the Home screen" off, and accept the prompt on the Fire TV.

## Privacy switches

Settings > Preferences > Privacy Settings: turn off Device Usage Data, Collect App Usage Data and
Interest-based Ads, and on Fire TV sets, Over-the-air Viewing Data. They sync with the Amazon account, so
change them in this menu.

To block ads for the whole home as well, see [Block ads for any TV](polaris-tv-network.md).
