# App launcher

[Leer en español](launcher.es.md) - [Back to the README](../../README.md)

The menu in the top-left corner opens every app you can use, and says what is
waiting in each.

Every picture is the real interface drawn with made-up people and data, and
follows your light or dark setting. Phone-sized versions are in
[docs/assets/media](../assets/media).

Unread chats and mail, and anything that needs an administrator, are counted
on the app they belong to and listed by name, so a click opens the one
conversation. Apps can be searched, kept as favorites and arranged by
dragging; the first ones also appear on the Overview. Apps are switched on per
account and per role, and the menu only lists the ones you can open.

## Personal or organization

The switch beside the app name, shown when the account belongs to an
organization, picks whose things you are looking at: your own, or an
organization's projects, spaces, files and domains. It is in every picture on
these pages.
