# Does it work on my TV?

[Leer en español](polaris-tv-compatibility.es.md) - [Back to the README](../../README.md)

If your TV runs Android TV or Google TV, version 8 or newer, yes. Your model does not need to be on the
list: open Settings > About on the TV, and if it says Android TV or Google TV with a version of 8 or more,
Polaris TV can be its Home screen.

## What you get, by TV

- **Home screen**: Android TV 8 or newer and Google TV, including Chromecast with Google TV, the Google TV
  Streamer, TV boxes and projectors. Polaris TV replaces the Home screen, can block ads and trackers on
  the TV itself (Android 9 or newer), and installs from the Polaris desktop app.
  [Set it up in three steps](polaris-tv-setup.md).
- **App**: Fire TV on Fire OS 6 or newer. Polaris TV opens as an app and Amazon keeps the Home button.
  [Fire TV guide](polaris-tv-fire.md). Apps for LG webOS and Samsung Tizen are coming; until they ship,
  those TVs are in the next group.
- **Network only**: Roku, Vizio, Hisense VIDAA, LG and Samsung for now, and anything else. No app, but the
  Network filter blocks ads and trackers for every TV at home once your router uses it for DNS.
  [Block ads for any TV](polaris-tv-network.md).

Whatever the TV, its own privacy switches cut most of the tracking:
[privacy switches by brand](polaris-tv-privacy.md).

## The list

The models below were tested by their owners with the open-source launcher Polaris TV is built from, and
published with their cleanup lists. They have not all been tried with Polaris TV yet, and each one says
so. Search by brand, model number or system; dashes and spaces in a model number do not matter.

## Not on the list?

Use the box under the list: pick what Settings > About shows and its version, and it answers with the
same rule the list uses.
