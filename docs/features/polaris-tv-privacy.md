# Privacy switches by brand

[Leer en español](polaris-tv-privacy.es.md) - [Back to the README](../../README.md)

Most smart TVs report what you watch (often called ACR, viewing information or Live Plus) and build an
advertising profile. These are the menus that turn it off, brand by brand. Menu paths change with
firmware, so yours may be worded a little differently; the menu names are given as the TV shows them in
English.

## The switches

Each brand below lists where the setting is and what to change, with the page it was read from.
