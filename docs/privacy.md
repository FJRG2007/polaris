# Privacy policy for the Polaris phone apps

[Leer en español](privacy.es.md)

This policy covers Polaris for Android and Polaris for iPhone. Each is a client:
it opens a Polaris server that you, or the organization you belong to, chose and
runs. The apps are published by the Polaris open-source project.

## What the apps send, and to whom

The apps send what you enter or share - your account details, messages, photos,
videos, voice messages, calls and files - only to the Polaris server whose
address you typed in. That server is run by you or your organization, and its
operator is responsible for what it keeps. The publisher of the apps receives
none of it.

The apps contain no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no
third-party tracking.

The Android app, when it was installed from a download rather than from a store,
asks GitHub whether a newer version is out. That request carries no personal
data. A copy installed from Google Play never makes it.

## What stays on the phone

The address of each server you add, its name, and the session that keeps you
signed in to it. Removing a server from the app deletes what the phone kept for
it.

## Permissions

- **Camera and microphone**: only when you take a photo or video, record a voice
  message or join a call.
- **Notifications**: to tell you about messages, calls and sign-in requests from
  your server.
- **Photos (iPhone)**: only to save a picture you chose to download.
- **Local network (iPhone)**: to reach a server on your own network.
- **Running in the background (Android)**: a connection to your own server keeps
  messages and calls arriving while the app is closed, since a self-hosted server
  cannot use Google's push service.

## Deleting your data

Your data lives on your Polaris server: delete it, or your account, there, or ask
whoever runs it. Uninstalling the app removes everything it kept on the phone.

## Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions about it:
[open an issue](https://github.com/FJRG2007/polaris/issues) on the project's
repository. Questions about data on a server go to whoever runs that server.
