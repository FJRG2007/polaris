Install on a TV

For Android TV and Google TV. On the TV, open Settings, About, press Build 7 times, then turn on USB debugging in Developer options, and Network debugging to install over the network.

Connect the TV to this computer with a USB cable. Close adb, Android Studio or scrcpy if they are open.

No TV found on this computer's USB ports. Check the cable and that USB debugging is on.

A backup of the TV's settings, its DNS included, is saved here first. Nothing is uninstalled or disabled, and debugging is turned off when it finishes.
